Revoke the application's access

A brand can remove the application at any time in the settings of the platform concerned, for example in the connected apps or app permissions section of Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, TikTok or Google. You can also email us to disconnect a platform, and we remove the stored access tokens for it.

Delete data

  • A connected brand: email us with the brand and platform. We delete the data received from that platform for that brand within 30 days, as described in the Privacy Policy.
  • Someone who commented on or messaged a connected brand: email us with the platform and the account name you used. We erase your comments and messages within 30 days.
