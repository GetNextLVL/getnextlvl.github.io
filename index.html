NextLVL Social Agents

NextLVL Social Agents is the internal social media management tool of NextLVL, operated by Almog Malul. Our team uses it to manage the social media accounts of the brands we work with. The first managed brand is Aignite; other brands that authorize us may be added later.

What it does

  • Publishes images and videos that a person on our team has chosen and approved to each brand's own Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, TikTok or YouTube account.
  • Collects comments on the brand's own posts so that our team can review them, and drafts reply suggestions that a person approves before anything is sent.
  • Reports how each brand's own posts perform.

How accounts are connected

Each brand authorizes each of its accounts separately through the platform's official sign-in and consent screen. The application never asks for passwords. A brand can revoke access at any time in that platform's settings.

Human approval

Nothing is published automatically. A person on our team reviews and approves every post and every reply.

Who can use it

Only our own team. The application is not offered to the public and has no sign-up.

Contact, revocation and deletion

Email getnextlvl@gmail.com. How to revoke access or have data deleted is explained on the Support page and in the Privacy Policy.
