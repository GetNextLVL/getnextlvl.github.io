Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026.

Who operates this application

NextLVL Social Agents is operated by Almog Malul, an individual, under the trading name NextLVL. NextLVL is not a registered company. Questions about this policy or your data: getnextlvl@gmail.com.

What the application does

NextLVL Social Agents is an internal tool our team uses to manage the social media accounts of the brands we work with. The first connected brand is Aignite; other brands may be connected later. Each brand connects its own accounts on Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, TikTok or YouTube separately, through that platform's own authorization screen. A person on our team reviews and approves every post before it is published. The application is not offered to the public and no one can sign up for it.

Information we access

  • Connected brand accounts. The account identifier and name, and the access tokens the platform issues when the brand authorizes the application.
  • Content we publish. The images, videos and captions a brand approves, and the identifiers and links the platform returns for each published post.
  • Comments and messages sent to a brand. The text of comments on the brand's own posts and, where a brand enables it, direct messages sent to the brand's account, with the sender's platform identifier, so that our team can review and answer them.
  • Performance figures. Counts such as reach, views, likes, comments, shares and saves for the brand's own posts, and the account's follower count.
  • Board and channel lists. The brand's own Pinterest boards and recent Pins, and its YouTube channel and uploads, read to confirm what was published.

We do not access the accounts of people who are not connected brands, other than the comments and messages they send to a connected brand.

How we use it

Only to publish content a person on our team has approved, to show our team the comments and messages that need an answer, to draft replies for a person to review, and to report how each brand's own posts perform. We do not sell or rent this information, do not use it for advertising, and do not build profiles of individuals.

Automated drafting

To draft reply and post suggestions, the text of an incoming comment, the brand's approved reference information and a summary of how the brand's own posts performed may be sent to an AI model provider, currently accessed through Nous Research's Nous Portal. Drafts are only suggestions: a person approves every reply and post before it is published.

Where it is stored and who processes it

Data is stored in a database on a server we rent from Hetzner Online GmbH in its Falkenstein data center in Germany. These services also process data for us:

  • Nous Research (Nous Portal) and the AI model it routes to: the drafting inputs described above.
  • Telegram: review notifications to our own team, including the caption and image of a post awaiting approval.
  • The connected platforms themselves: Meta (Instagram and Facebook), Pinterest, TikTok and Google (YouTube), each under its own terms and privacy policy.

Some of these services may process data outside Germany and outside the European Economic Area.

How long we keep it

  • Direct message text: erased automatically 30 days after it is received.
  • YouTube statistics: re-checked at least every 30 days, and deleted automatically when a video is no longer available, has not been re-checked for 30 days, or six days after authorization is lost.
  • Comment text, commenter identifiers, published post records and other performance figures: kept while the brand uses the application. They are not deleted automatically; they are deleted on request as described below.
  • Access tokens: kept until the brand disconnects the platform or the token expires.

Revoking access and deleting data

Revoking access. A brand can revoke the application's access at any time in the settings of the platform concerned. We can also disconnect a platform on request, which removes the stored access tokens for that brand and platform.

Deleting a brand's data. On request, or when a brand revokes access or stops using the application, we delete the data received from that platform for that brand: comment and message text and sender identifiers are erased, statistics are deleted, and the platform identifiers of published posts are removed. Records of posts still being published are cleaned once they finish. We do this within 30 days. The posts themselves stay on the platform until the brand deletes them there.

Deleting your own comments or messages. If you commented on or messaged a connected brand and want that removed from our system, email us with the platform and the account name you used. We erase your comments and messages for that brand within 30 days.

Backups. A backup made before a deletion can still contain the deleted data. We replace or delete such backups within 30 days of the deletion.

You can also ask us what information we hold about you. Requests go to getnextlvl@gmail.com.

Security

Access tokens are kept in protected configuration on the server, outside the database and source code. Only our own team's Telegram accounts can approve content. Media is shared with the platforms only through signed links that expire, by default after 15 minutes. No method of storage or transfer is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

We will update this page when the application's data practices change, and change the date above.
